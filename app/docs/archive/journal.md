## Current state

As of 2026-10-07 (branch `feature/job-matching-engine-baseline`, uncommitted work). The full jobs and companies files were audited, a seeded 30,000-job sample was prepared, and three local retrieval methods (keyword overlap, TF-IDF cosine, MiniLM embeddings) were compared on fictional profiles. All relevance judgments so far are automated, not human-validated. **Embeddings are the provisional choice for further validation, not an established broad winner.** The bounded fresh-profile validation is complete; human review, skills and adjacency features, timing analysis, company-file enrichment, and backend integration remain pending.
